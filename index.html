My Dearest Darla,

I am writing this to you with a heavy heart, complete humility, and tears of regret. I am so deeply, truly, and profoundly sorry for how I misbehaved with you.

There is no excuse, no justification, and no explanation that could ever defend the way I treated you. When I look back at my words and my actions, I feel sick inside knowing that the pain in your eyes came from me—the one person whose sole purpose should be to protect your smile and shelter your heart.

You did not deserve that. Not even for a second. You are the gentlest, most loving, and most caring woman in the world, and taking your kindness for granted or letting my own frustration spill over onto you was thoughtless, hurtful, and wrong. I own my mistake completely. I take full responsibility without shifting blame, because you deserve honesty, humility, and genuine accountability.

"Darla, you are my whole world. You are all I have. In this lifetime, nothing matters more to me than your happiness, your comfort, and knowing that you are safe in my love."

When things are not right between us, my entire world feels cold and off-balance. I can’t eat right, I can’t think straight, and every quiet moment reminds me of how much I cherish you and how terrified I am of causing you pain. You aren’t just my wife—you are my best friend, my soulmate, my peace, and my anchor. Without you, I am lost.

I love you so much, Darla. I love you far beyond what simple words can hold, and I hate myself for ever making you feel unappreciated, disrespected, or hurt.
